# Agent-authored external reasoning: cycle 3 design

Human authorization: "Build that out. Run it against controls. Have fun." Goal: native reasoning produces external state that enables fresh downstream reasoning; do not frame as extending native capacity.

Three preregistered seeds 3101, 3102, 3103; prime modulus 11. Hidden invertible affine map from two coordinates (x,y) to (u,v). Producer sees three noncollinear observations and a declared affine family, infers two coefficient triples. Consumer sees a new target (u,v) and must invert the map to obtain (x,y). Initial producer never sees target. Revision producer sees the actual intact consumer cache (possibly wrong coordinates), but not target u,v or evaluator answers; target-free claim applies only to the initial producer. Consumer has fresh context and cannot see producer transcript. Store validates shape/version/references only, never mathematics. Offline evaluator scores only after raw responses saved, supplies no semantic feedback. Malformed producer: preserve rejection, do not synthesize replacement or retry; omit dependent trials and report attrition. Structurally accepted but semantically wrong states still receive downstream trials. Malformed revision omits only its revised consumer. No correctness gating.

Per case: producer; four fresh consumer arms (intact agent state, omitted relation, altered u intercept +1 mod11, raw original observations); revision producer with authoritative replacement u observations and prior state, preserve v and invalidate dependent answer; fresh revision consumer. Planned21 calls. Arm scheduling deterministic shuffled seed and logged. State structural acceptance does not imply correctness. Altered-state evaluator scores both true-world correctness and conditional correctness under altered relation. Omission expected undetermined, not incorrect reasoning. Raw-evidence arm can reproduce producer computation; call/token budgets not equal. Three cases cannot support population claims.

Revision changes only u intercept by +2, provides sufficient fresh observations and declares old u evidence superseded. Consumer receives original target and revised state; producer sees prior cached downstream answer, must invalidate it and preserve untouched v. New relation must change solution. Explicit invalidation instruction is a limitation, not autonomous dependency discovery. No semantic checker between producer/consumer. JSON response state may be persisted opaquely with safe compressed encoding; no explanatory prose needed.

Acceptance: independent enumerative evaluator and generator tests; wrong but structurally valid relation accepted by store; stale/malformed patches rejected without state change; saved exact prompts/raw outputs/hashes; frozen source/protocol before subjects; fresh Luna-low subjects; independent audit and scoped checkpoint. No production repository edits or cycle1/2 evidence changes.
