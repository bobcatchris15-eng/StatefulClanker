# Blackboard protocol
Schema v1. Independent role files are authoritative for their authors' claims; INDEX is navigation only.
Entries: claim ID, assertion, evidence URL/artifact, evidence status, applicability, strongest objection, proposed falsifier, response, unresolved issues.
Peer communication explicitly authorized by user. Preserve consequential messages or summaries on disk; transcripts alone are not durable evidence.
Experimental pre-registration must precede subject responses. Ground truth belongs to evaluator; access restriction is procedural, not technically enforced. No subject may see earlier subject responses. Subjects may only read listed fixtures and state, and write assigned responses. Record actual violations.
Research roles: advocate, skeptic, experimentalist, technical/formal analyst, independent methods validator. They may be rehydrated sequentially; concurrency cap three alongside root. Fresh experiment subjects do not substitute for independent research roles.
